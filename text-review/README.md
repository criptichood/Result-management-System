## 🎯 Quick Project Summary

- **Institution**: Federal University of Agriculture, Zuru (FUAZ), Kebbi State, Nigeria.
- **Project Type**: Enterprise Academic Information System (Undergraduate School Project).
- **Primary Objective**: Replace manual, error-prone paper grade computation and uncoordinated result handling with a transparent, role-governed, tamper-evident digital portal.
- **Key Highlight**: Full separation of concerns between **Lecturer grading**, **Examiner moderation**, **Senate/Admin oversight**, and **Student instant verification**.
