# 05. Course Management, Workload Allocation & Reports

## 1. Course Management & Curriculum Rules

The Administrator maintains the central curriculum database:
- **Course Code**: Standard alphanumeric code (e.g., `CSC101`, `MTH101`, `BIO201`).
- **Credit Units**: Numerical unit weight (typically 1 to 4 units).
- **Academic Level**: 100L, 200L, 300L, 400L.
- **Semester**: 1st (Harmattan/Alpha) or 2nd (Rain/Omega) semester.
- **Course Nature**:
  - **Core Departmental Course**: Compulsory for students in that department to graduate.
  - **Borrowed / Elective Course**: Offered by external servicing departments (e.g., Mathematics or General Studies for Computer Science candidates).

---

## 2. Lecturer Workload Allocation

- The Administrator or Head of Department assigns individual courses to verified faculty members.
- **Workload Safeguards**: Prevents overloading lecturers with excessive credit units per semester.
- **Co-Lecturer Assignment**: Supports primary course leads alongside supporting examiners for large multi-department classes.

---

## 3. Broadsheets & Official Documents

### 1. Departmental Master Broadsheet (Senate Format)
- Formatted horizontally according to institutional university standards.
- Columns display Student Matric Number, Full Name, individual course columns (displaying CA, Exam, Total, and Grade), followed by semester summary columns:
  - **TCR** (Total Credit Registered)
  - **TCE** (Total Credit Earned)
  - **TWP** (Total Weighted Points)
  - **GPA** (Semester Grade Point Average)
  - **CGPA** (Cumulative Grade Point Average)
  - **Remark / Degree Class** (Pass, Carryover Course list, First Class, etc.)
- Fully printable directly using `@media print` styling without browser interface clutter.

### 2. Official Student Result Slip
- Generated per semester or cumulatively.
- Features:
  - University Crest and Official Header ("Federal University of Agriculture, Zuru").
  - Complete Student Biodata (Matriculation Number, Department, Faculty/College, Level, Academic Session).
  - High-precision table of all enrolled courses, credit units, scores, grades, and grade points.
  - Performance badge summary (TCR, TCE, TWP, GPA, CGPA).
  - Registrar / Dean signature block and official date.
  - **Digital Verification QR / Tracking Code**: Unique cryptographic tracking hash (e.g., `FUAZ-SRMS-STU01-X9A2`) for fraud mitigation.

### 3. Official Statement of Academic Result & Transcript
- Complete academic history from 100L to final year.
- Ready for official postgraduate applications, National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) mobilization clearance, or employer verification.
